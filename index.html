Monday

A personal assistant for an Android phone and a Wear OS watch.

Monday is a private, single-user application. It is not distributed on any app store and is not offered as a service to anyone else. It runs on its author's own devices and reads its author's own data.

Health data

Monday can read electrocardiogram readings recorded by the user's own watch through the Google Health API, so that beat-to-beat intervals and heart rate variability can be measured from the raw waveform rather than taken on trust from a summary figure.

Privacy

Health data is read on the user's own device and is not transmitted anywhere. See the privacy policy.
